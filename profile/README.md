# McKittrick LLC

An independent studio that builds and runs small, focused apps and websites. Founded and run by [Micah McKittrick](https://github.com/micahmckittrick).

McKittrick LLC is the parent company of two brands:

- **[McKittrick Studios](https://www.mckittrickstudios.com):** apps and websites
- **[NubblShop](https://www.nubblshop.com):** makers of The Fuzzy, a fidget designed for adult sensory seekers

## Products

| Product | What it does | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Clashboard** | War, league, and river-race tracking for Clash of Clans and Clash Royale | [Web](https://clashboard.gg) · [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/clashboard-clan-manager/id6794932081) |
| **AnimeGems** | Anime and manga discovery, plus where to watch | [Web](https://www.animegems.com) |
| **Checkbook** | Manual transaction tracking with cleared and pending balances | [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/checkbook-simple-tracking/id6762422098) |
| **Simple Business Manager** | A private, local-first binder for business records, documents, and dates | [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/simple-business-manager/id6816590086) |
| **Can They Have It?** | Food lookup and daily care tracking for pet owners | [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/can-they-have-it/id6794688634) |
| **Tarot Card Draw** | Free readings, all 78 card meanings, lessons and quizzes | [Web](https://tarotcarddraw.com) |
